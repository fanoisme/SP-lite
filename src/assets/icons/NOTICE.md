# Material icon assets

The SVG files in this directory are selected Material icons copied into the app
as versioned local assets.

## Primary source — Material Symbols

Most icons are Material Symbols, weight 400, outlined style, from
`@material-symbols/svg-400`.

Material Symbols are provided under the Apache License 2.0.
Source: https://github.com/marella/material-symbols

## Fallback source — Material Icons

Five icons absent from `@material-symbols/svg-400` are copied from
`@material-design-icons/svg` (outlined):

- `auto_awesome`
- `auto_fix_high`
- `expand_less`
- `expand_more`
- `file_download`

Material Icons are provided under the Apache License 2.0.
Source: https://github.com/marella/material-design-icons
